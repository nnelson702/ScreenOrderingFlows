How to think about openness and UV blockage

Openness describes how much open area exists in the mesh. In general, more openness means more airflow and outward visibility. Denser solar screens trade some openness for greater solar blockage.

UV blockage is the configured product specification for how much ultraviolet light the material is designed to block. It is not the same thing as a guarantee of indoor temperature reduction.

Las Vegas tip: A west- or south-facing window exposed to intense afternoon sun may benefit from a denser solar screen, while shaded windows may be better served by standard fiberglass or a more open material.
