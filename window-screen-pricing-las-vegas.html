Screen Ordering / Window Screen Pricing

How Much Does a Replacement Window Screen Cost in Las Vegas?

Replacement screen pricing depends on the finished width and height, frame profile and screen material. Helpful ACE lets you configure the actual screen you need and see your quote online instead of waiting for an appointment or generic estimate.

Real price example: A 36" wide × 30" high window screen using standard fiberglass mesh and an eligible standard frame profile prices at $34.98 before applicable sales tax in the current Helpful ACE ordering tool.

What is included in that $34.98 example?

  • A complete new replacement window screen frame
  • Standard fiberglass screen mesh
  • A supported standard frame profile
  • Up to 6 standard window-screen hardware pieces at no added charge
  • Custom fabrication to the measurements submitted through the ordering tool

This is a finished replacement screen, not a repair or rescreening of an old frame.

How the example price is calculated

The current standard fiberglass pricing rate for eligible standard window-screen frame profiles is $0.53 per priced half-perimeter inch. The live tool prices the example as:

36 + 30 = 66 priced inches
66 × $0.53 = $34.98

The live configurator performs this calculation automatically for the measurements and eligible frame/material combination you choose.

What changes the price of a replacement screen?

Width and Height

Larger screens use more frame and mesh, so dimensions are one of the main factors in the final price.

Screen Material

Standard fiberglass is the lowest-priced material in many supported window-screen configurations. Aluminum, pet-resistant, VIMCO and solar-screen materials use different rates.

Frame Profile

Most eligible standard fiberglass frame profiles use the same rate, while specialty profiles such as standoff configurations can use a different rate.

Quantity

Each unique screen is priced as its own line item. Identical screens can be entered with a higher quantity.

No appointment required to see your price

If you know the finished outside dimensions and can identify the frame and hardware you need, you can build the screen online and see your actual quote without scheduling someone to visit first.

Use our window-screen measurement guide before starting your quote.

Pickup and delivery

Pickup is available through the local Helpful ACE fulfillment network. Local delivery is a separate fulfillment option and is currently enabled by the ordering tool only when the screen subtotal is at least $35 and the address is within the supported delivery radius of the selected store.

The $34.98 example above is therefore just below the current $35 subtotal required to enable delivery by itself.
