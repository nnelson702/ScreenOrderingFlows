Choose the screen that fits the job

Fiberglass Screen

A standard vinyl-coated 18x16 insect mesh with approximately 45% openness. It is the practical general-purpose choice for everyday residential window screens.

Aluminum Screen

A firmer metal mesh option for customers who prefer aluminum screening. The current ordering program offers the available aluminum configuration shown in the tool.

Pet-Resistant Screen

Vinyl-coated material designed for greater resistance to pet traffic, tearing and punctures. The current PET option is available in black where supported by the selected configuration.

VIMCO 20x30

A tighter 20x30 solar insect screen with approximately 36% openness and 65% UV blockage, useful when smaller-insect protection and solar reduction both matter.

Solar 70 / Solar 90

Vinyl-coated fiberglass solar options with approximately 75% or 90% UV blockage. Higher blockage means a denser screen and lower openness.

SunTex 80 / SunTex 90

Vinyl-coated polyester solar-screen choices with approximately 80% or 90% UV blockage for sun-exposed windows.

Frame and hardware options

Window screens can require different frame profiles, colors and retention hardware. The ordering tool walks you through the frame choices available for your screen, then lets you place hardware such as leaf springs, pull tabs, bale clips, plungers and tension springs on the correct side of the frame.

Important: Your replacement screen is built from the information you submit. Confirm finished outside width, finished outside height, frame profile, hardware type and hardware location before placing the order.

Professionally manufactured

Helpful ACE sends paid orders to ScreenFab, our screen-manufacturing partner. ScreenFab specializes in wholesale window screens, solar screens, patio screen doors, frame and screen hardware. Finished orders return through our local store network on our regular vendor delivery cycle.
