Choose the right screen material

The live ordering tool includes standard fiberglass, aluminum, pet-resistant screen, VIMCO 20x30, Solar 70, Solar 90, SunTex 80 and SunTex 90 in the configurations where those products are supported.

Solar-screen choices trade some openness for increased UV blockage, while standard fiberglass emphasizes everyday insect screening and airflow. Pet-resistant material is designed for higher-traffic openings and greater resistance to tearing and punctures.

Screen replacement FAQ

Do you repair old screen frames?

No. This program is designed for complete replacement window screens and patio screen doors built from the measurements and options you provide.

Who makes the screens?

Our replacement screens are manufactured for us by ScreenFab, a wholesale screen manufacturer that produces window screens, solar screens and patio screen doors.

How long does it take?

Orders are submitted to production after payment. Finished screens return to our stores on our regular weekly vendor delivery cycle. Timing can vary depending on when the order is placed and the vendor production cycle.

Can I get my screen delivered to my home?

Eligible orders can choose local delivery when the ordering tool confirms that the order and delivery location meet the delivery requirements.

Do you offer pet-resistant screen?

Yes, when available for the selected configuration. Pet-resistant screen is designed for higher-traffic openings and better resistance to tearing and punctures.

Do you offer solar screen?

Yes. Several solar-screen materials are available with different openness and UV-blockage specifications.

Can I order more than one screen?

Yes. Add each unique screen as a separate line item. If several screens are exactly the same, increase the quantity for that configuration.

Do I need to bring my old screen to the store?

Not necessarily. If you can accurately determine the finished dimensions, frame details and hardware needed, you can submit the order online. An existing screen can be very useful as a reference when identifying hardware and confirming finished size.
