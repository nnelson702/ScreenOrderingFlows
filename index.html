Custom Window & Patio Screen Replacement in Las Vegas

Need a complete new screen? Helpful ACE makes it easier to order custom replacement window screens and patio sliding screen doors for homes across Las Vegas and Henderson. This service is for replacement screens, not repair or rescreening of an existing frame.

Grab a tape measure and we'll walk you through, step by step, how to submit an accurate order for custom window and patio door screens.

  • Guided questions for measurements, materials, and hardware.
  • Automatic connection to your nearest Helpful ACE store.
  • A clear quote with pickup and eligible delivery options.

Complete replacement screens built to your measurements

Use the online tool to create a quote for one screen or several. Each screen is entered as its own line item so you can specify the dimensions, frame, material and hardware needed for that opening.

Replacement window screens

Enter the finished outside width and height to the nearest 1/8 inch, then choose the available frame profile and color, screen material, hardware placement, and optional crossbar details.

Patio sliding screen doors

The same tool supports complete patio sliding screen door replacement with door-specific selections for dimensions, rollers and handle orientation.

How the screen ordering process works

  1. Enter your contact information and select the Helpful ACE store serving your order.
  2. Add each replacement window screen or patio sliding screen door to your quote.
  3. Review measurements, materials, hardware selections and pricing before submitting.
  4. Follow the payment and fulfillment options provided with your quote to place the order.

Screen replacement FAQ

Do you repair or rescreen existing window screens?

No. This ordering service is for complete replacement window screens and patio sliding screen doors rather than repair or rescreening of an existing frame.

Can I order a complete new window screen?

Yes. The tool guides you through finished dimensions, frame selection, screen material, hardware and other details needed to quote a complete replacement window screen.

Can I order a patio sliding screen door?

Yes. Patio sliding screen doors can be quoted through the same ordering tool with door-specific roller, handle and measurement selections.

How accurately should I measure my screen?

Enter the finished outside width and height to the nearest 1/8 inch. Please confirm all measurements and hardware selections before ordering because the screen will be built from the information you submit.

Is delivery available?

Delivery may be available depending on the order and delivery location. The ordering flow will calculate the fulfillment options available for your quote.
